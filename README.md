# S.W.E.E.T. TEAms
**Making Our Youth as S.W.E.E.T. as Our Tea**

S.W.E.E.T. TEAms is a web-based Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) tool designed for youth in grades 6–12.  
It uses interactive, conversation-based questions to build meaningful connections, emotional awareness, and teamwork skills.

## 🎲 How It Works

1. Youth sit or stand in a circle
2. A ball numbered 1–25 is tossed
3. The catcher enters the number into the app
4. A question appears based on:
   - Age level
   - Selected category
5. Youth answer and discussion follows
6. Play continues

---

## 🏆 Competitive Mode: *How Well Do You Know Me?*

- Youth are divided into **two teams**
- If only two youth are playing, each represents a team
- The catcher answers a question about the thrower
- Correct answers earn **1 point for the team**
- Incorrect answers lead to learning and sharing
- Focus is on connection, not winning
